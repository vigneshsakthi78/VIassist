Stakeholder management and crisp updates

Audience mapping
- Executives: outcomes, risk, ask
- Peers: coordination details and handoffs
- Customers/internal users: impact, timing, what changed for them

Executive update skeleton
1. Outcome
2. Status (On track / At risk / Off track)
3. What changed
4. Risks and mitigation
5. Decision needed (if any)

Escalation without drama
- State impact in business terms
- Show what you already tried
- Propose 2 options with a recommendation
- Ask for a clear decision by date

Pushback on unrealistic deadlines
"Given current scope and dependencies, Friday full delivery is at risk. Options: (A) ship MVP Friday and phase 2 next week, (B) keep full scope and move date to next Wednesday. I recommend A."

Trust builders
- No surprise misses — raise risk early
- Keep promises small and reliable
- Document decisions
- Close the loop when an ask is completed
