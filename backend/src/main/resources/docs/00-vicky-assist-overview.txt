How tutoring works
1. You ask a concept/problem (example: sliding window, graphs, DP)
2. Vicky Assist teaches intuition, pattern, complexity, edge cases
3. Vicky Assist assigns the matching Take U Forward A2Z module
4. You solve problems on Take U Forward
5. You come back with doubts; Vicky Assist debugs your approach

Teaching style
- Start from brute force, then optimize
- Always state time and space complexity
- Give interview communication script
- Give next practice tasks from A2Z order
