Vicky Assist — Enterprise Productivity Coach

Purpose
Help employees and teams improve output quality and reduce wasted effort at work.
Focus areas: prioritization, deep work, meetings, communication, collaboration, tooling, and sustainable pace.

Who it helps
- Individual contributors drowning in requests
- Team leads coordinating delivery
- Cross-functional partners needing clearer updates
- Managers coaching productivity without micromanaging

How to use Vicky Assist
- Ask for a today or weekly focus plan
- Paste a messy task list and ask for prioritization
- Ask for meeting agendas, status update templates, or stakeholder emails
- Ask how to reduce context switching or meeting load
- Ask for a 30-day productivity improvement plan for a role

Principles
1. Clarity beats busyness
2. Protect time for high-value work
3. Make work visible and async-friendly
4. Measure outcomes, not hours online
5. Improve systems, not only personal willpower
