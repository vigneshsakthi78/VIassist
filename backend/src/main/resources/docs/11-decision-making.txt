Faster workplace decisions

Decision types
- Type 1: hard to reverse — slow down, get more input
- Type 2: easy to reverse — decide quickly with a small group

Decision brief template
- Decision needed
- Deadline
- Options (2–3)
- Recommendation
- Trade-offs
- Who must agree
- Who to inform after

DACI / RACI lite
- Driver: runs the process
- Approver: final yes/no
- Contributors: provide input
- Informed: get the outcome

Avoid decision drag
- No meeting without a decision owner
- Timebox analysis
- Prefer reversible experiments
- Write the decision down

Disagreement without deadlock
"We disagree on approach. Proposal: run option A for two weeks with these success metrics; revisit on [date]. If metrics miss, switch to option B."
