MACK DMS — How to use (overview)

What is DMS?
DMS (Document Management System) in MACK / NiBiKi is the controlled library for company manuals, SMS documents, circulars, bulletins, forms, and related controlled information. It is commonly accessed under areas such as Manual & Info Sharing and SMS & Circulars.

Who uses it
- Shore office users: create, revise, distribute, and control documents
- Vessel / shipboard users (e.g. SAILS side): receive, view, acknowledge, and use the latest controlled documents
- Support / admins: access mapping (PAM / User Level Security), distribution, and troubleshooting

What DMS is for
- Keep one controlled source of truth for SMS / manuals / circulars
- Distribute the right documents to the right vessels or offices
- Preserve version control and auditability of document changes
- Support audits, vetting, and management-system compliance

Important
Company-specific screens, folder names, and permissions vary by customer configuration. Always follow your company SMS / document-control procedure if it differs from this general guide.
