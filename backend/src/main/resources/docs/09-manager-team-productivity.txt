Manager playbook: improve team productivity without micromanaging

Manager leverage points
- Clear priorities and trade-offs
- Healthy meeting load
- Fast decisions
- Skill growth and unblocking
- Psychological safety for raising risks early

1:1 agenda that improves output
- Wins and energy
- Top priorities and risks
- Where I can remove friction
- Career / skill growth note
- Commitments both ways

Team operating system
- Weekly priorities visible to all
- Single intake for new requests
- Explicit WIP limits
- Retrospectives that change one process each time

Anti-micromanagement
- Manage outcomes and interfaces, not every keystroke
- Ask for demos and written updates on cadence
- Intervene on blockers and conflicting priorities
- Coach judgment with questions, not only instructions

Capacity conversations
When load is too high, help the team choose:
- Stop
- Delay
- Shrink scope
- Add help
Do not silently accept impossible plans.
