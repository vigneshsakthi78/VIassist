MACK DMS — Distribution, vessel targeting, and sync awareness

Why distribution matters
Publishing in the office is not always enough. Vessel users only see documents that are distributed/synced to their vessel according to configuration and connectivity.

Office checklist before/after distribute
1. Confirm correct document and revision
2. Confirm correct folder/category
3. Select intended distribution vessels only
4. Distribute / publish
5. Verify one sample vessel can open the document
6. If a vessel was removed from distribution, confirm it no longer retains an unintended active copy per company process

Common distribution problems
- Newly created folder/documents not visible on a vessel
- Vessel missing from distribution list after save
- Old attachment still shown after office update
- Quick View blank on vessel but OK in office
- Forms/checklists cannot be viewed or downloaded on SAILS

What to capture for support
- Company / fleet
- Vessel name
- Module (e.g. SMS & Circulars, Manual & Info Sharing)
- Document ID and title
- Folder path
- Office result vs vessel result
- Time of distribute/sync attempt
- Screenshot of distribution list and vessel view

Practical tip
If only one vessel is affected, suspect distribution targeting or vessel sync. If many vessels are affected, suspect publish/content/attachment or a wider sync issue.
