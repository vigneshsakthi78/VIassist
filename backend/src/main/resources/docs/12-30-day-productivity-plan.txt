30-day employee productivity improvement plan

Week 1 — Visibility
- List recurring meetings and mark keep / shorten / cut
- Create a personal kanban
- Define top 3 outcomes for the month
- Start end-of-day shutdown notes

Week 2 — Focus
- Add 3–5 recurring deep-work blocks
- Batch chat/email
- Replace one status meeting with async update
- Practice saying no with options

Week 3 — Systems
- Build templates for status, handoff, and meeting notes
- Clarify definition of done with your team
- Automate one repetitive task
- Reduce WIP to improve finish rate

Week 4 — Stakeholder clarity
- Send crisp weekly update to key partners
- Escalate one lingering blocker with options
- Review metrics: focus hours, finished priorities, meeting hours
- Choose 2 habits to keep permanently

Success checks
- Fewer dropped commitments
- Faster cycle time on important work
- Clearer stakeholder trust
- More predictable weeks
