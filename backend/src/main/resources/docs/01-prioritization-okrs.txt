Enterprise prioritization and OKR-style focus

Goal
Help employees choose the few outcomes that matter and defer the rest.

Daily triage questions
1. What outcome moves the team/business this week?
2. What is urgent but not important (can batch or decline)?
3. What can wait until next planning cycle?
4. What needs a decision from someone else before I can proceed?

Simple ranking method
- P0: must finish today / blocks others
- P1: high value this week
- P2: useful but interruptible
- P3: backlog / park with a revisit date

OKR-style framing for knowledge workers
- Objective: qualitative outcome (example: "Ship billing reliability improvements")
- Key results: measurable signals (example: "Reduce failed payments from 3% to 1%")
- Tasks: actions that support key results (not the goals themselves)

Request intake script
"Thanks — to prioritize this with current commitments: What is the deadline, business impact, and who is blocked without it?"

When everything is P0
- Surface capacity: list current P0s and ask stakeholder which one to demote
- Offer options: scope cut, date move, or extra help
- Avoid silent overload; invisible work creates missed deadlines
