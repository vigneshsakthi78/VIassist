MACK DMS — FAQ and support ticket quality

Source: combined from SharePoint DMS Training Material + support practice

FAQ
Q: How do I find a document quickly?
A: Use Search or Advanced Search by Document Title, Content/Page, or Document ID.

Q: Where do SMS attachments come from?
A: Training guidance: SMS attachments via Document Manager; Circulars may upload from local folder.

Q: Document missing on vessel after office publish?
A: Confirm Published status, visibility/distribution, then check Data Sync Report / SSC Comparison Report for mismatches.

Q: What is Cancelled in Trash?
A: For published circulars overridden by a new version, the old version can appear under Cancelled.

Q: How do templates help?
A: Create templates in Template Master, then Insert Template while editing a page for consistent SMS/Circular formatting.

Q: How do conditional variables help?
A: Show different text/details based on conditions such as Vessel Type.

Support ticket template
1. Customer / company / environment
2. Shore or vessel (vessel name if ship)
3. Module / screen (DMS tree, Document Manager, SSC report, etc.)
4. Document ID + title + folder path
5. Expected vs actual
6. Workflow status (draft/review/approve/published)
7. Screenshots
8. Sync/SSC evidence if shore vs ship mismatch
