MACK DMS — Common issues, FAQ, and support ticket quality

FAQ
Q: Where do I start in DMS?
A: Manual & Info Sharing / SMS & Circulars (as configured). Search by Document ID when known.

Q: Quick View is blank
A: Note Document ID, module, office vs vessel, browser. Try another document. Then raise support with screenshots.

Q: Vessel cannot see a newly published document
A: Check distribution vessel list and sync. Confirm office can see it first.

Q: Attachment looks old after an update
A: Confirm the correct revision was saved/distributed. Check comparison/report tools if available. Raise support with before/after evidence.

Q: User manuals flagged or blocked
A: Security scanners may flag PDFs. Provide exact file names/Document IDs and whether office and vessel behave differently.

Q: How do I ask Vicky Assist for help?
A: Example prompts:
- "How do I find a document in DMS by Document ID?"
- "Steps to distribute an SMS circular to selected vessels"
- "Checklist when Quick View is blank on vessel"
- "Draft a support ticket for missing DMS document on SAILS"

Support ticket template
1. Module: (SMS & Circulars / Manual & Info Sharing / Bulletin / other)
2. Environment: (Prod / Test)
3. Company / vessel
4. Document ID + title + folder path
5. Expected result
6. Actual result
7. Office vs vessel behavior
8. Timestamp
9. Screenshots / HAR if performance or page rendering issue

Raise tickets in the correct company support desk project, with clear priority based on operational impact.
