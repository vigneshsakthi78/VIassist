Sustainable productivity and energy management

Sustainable pace beats heroic sprints that create defects and burnout.

Signals of overload
- Constant context switching
- Skipping breaks and lunch
- Rising mistakes
- Irritability in collaboration
- Work expanding into every evening by default

Recovery practices compatible with enterprise life
- Real breaks between meeting clusters
- Lunch away from laptop when possible
- End-of-day shutdown ritual
- Protect sleep around on-call or release windows
- Use PTO instead of quiet quitting on the job

Workload negotiation script
"I can deliver A and B this week at high quality. Adding C means we delay B or reduce scope of A. Which trade-off do you prefer?"

Focus on controllable inputs
Employees often cannot remove all meetings, but they can:
- Batch communication
- Clarify acceptance criteria
- Escalate conflicts early
- Keep a visible priority list

Health and performance are linked
Encourage habits that keep cognition sharp: movement, hydration, reasonable hours, and asking for help before a crisis.
